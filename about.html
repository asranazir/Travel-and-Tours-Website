Who We Are

Welcome to Wander Pakistan, a Travel and Tourism Website developed as a beginner-friendly web development project. Our aim is to provide visitors with useful information about beautiful destinations in Pakistan, travel tips, package details, accommodation and food preferences. We designed this website using HTML, CSS and JavaScript to make travel information easy to explore and understand.
